A dynamic 3D celestial mechanics and solar system simulation developed for UCLA CS 174A (Computer Graphics), focusing on realistic lighting physics, custom GLSL shader pipelines, and interactive planetary hierarchy transformations.

## Key Features

- **Custom GLSL Shader Pipeline:** Implemented custom WebGL shaders including Gouraud shading (per-vertex lighting calculations), Phong shading (per-fragment specular and diffuse calculations), and a custom multi-banded Ring Shader with procedural frequency-based color striping.
- **Procedural Solar Animation:** Simulated a pulsating coronal Sun with dynamic ambient illumination, color modulation, and point light generation that organically illuminates orbiting planetary bodies.
- **Hierarchical Coordinate Transformations:** Architected orbital mechanics utilizing matrix stack transformations (`Mat4`), simulating orbital radii, rotation periods, planetary axial tilts, and multi-body moon orbits around planets.
- **Interactive Camera Controls:** Real-time matrix attachment allowing the user to seamlessly anchor the camera to planetary bodies or freely navigate the orbital system.

A mathematical modeling and predictive analytics investigation developed for UCLA Math 142 (Mathematical Modeling). The project analyzes historical Summer Olympic Games datasets spanning 1896 through present day to examine host-nation competitive advantage, sport-specific nation improvements, and projections for the Los Angeles 2028 Games.

## Key Features

- **Host-Nation Advantage Quantification:** Built multivariable linear regression models isolating the statistically significant coefficient of host-nation status across total medal counts, rank changes, and event appearances.
- **Event-Level Competitiveness Analysis:** Dissected medal share trajectories to uncover nations demonstrating statistically significant positive or negative performance trends in specific sporting disciplines.
- **LA 2028 Medal Forecast:** Formulated empirical projection models to estimate nation-by-nation medal tallies and podium distribution for the upcoming Los Angeles 2028 Summer Olympic Games.
- **Relational Data Cleaning:** Processed and joined multiple historical relational tables (`summerOly_athletes`, `summerOly_hosts`, `summerOly_medal_counts`, `summerOly_programs`) addressing country renaming, team-sport medal counting rules, and missing historical records.
